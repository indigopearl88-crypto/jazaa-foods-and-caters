Our story

It started under the open sky.

Jazaa foods and caters began with one simple idea: that great food and warm hospitality can turn any gathering into something special. We started by taking our kitchen to lawns, farmhouses and homes across Islamabad, Rawalpindi & Expressway, serving families under the open sky.

Weddings and indoor functions followed, with décor, stages and event planning built around the kitchen, so families could book one team for the whole day.

Today we cook in our own kitchens and serve wherever you are: your venue, home, farmhouse, office or campus, from a family dinner to a walima of thousands. Daigs go out to homes across Islamabad, Rawalpindi & Expressway, and can be booked directly with our team.
